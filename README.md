# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

> **An end-to-end Data Analytics project using Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI to understand customer purchasing behavior and support data-driven business decisions.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer behavior is one of the most valuable assets for any retail business. Understanding how customers shop, what influences their purchasing decisions, and which customer segments generate the most value helps organizations improve marketing strategies, increase customer loyalty, and maximize revenue.

In this project, I analyzed customer shopping data to identify purchasing trends, customer segments, spending patterns, and the impact of discounts, subscriptions, and product ratings on sales.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow—from data cleaning and transformation to SQL-based business analysis and interactive dashboard development.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A retail company wanted to answer questions such as:

- Who are our most valuable customers?
- Which products perform the best?
- Are discounts really increasing sales?
- Do subscription members spend more?
- Which customer groups should marketing focus on?
- How can we improve customer retention?

The objective was to convert raw customer transaction data into meaningful business insights.

# 📈 Key Business Insights

Some important findings from the analysis include:

- Customer purchasing behavior varies significantly across demographics.
- Subscription customers contribute differently compared to non-subscribers.
- Certain products are highly dependent on discounts.
- Repeat customers represent an opportunity for loyalty programs.
- Product ratings influence purchasing behavior.
- Customer segmentation enables more targeted marketing campaigns.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Strengthen customer loyalty programs.
- Promote subscription benefits.
- Optimize discount strategies instead of applying blanket discounts.
- Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customer segments.
- Highlight top-rated products during promotions.
- Use customer behavior insights for personalized marketing.
